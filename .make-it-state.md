## Previous Session (2026-06-16 → 17, resume-it → wrap-it)
- Investigated the "full-pane selection wash on epic finish" bug. No code changed — diagnosis only.
- Traced it into SwiftTerm's selection model. High-confidence mechanism: the wash is a real
  text selection (inverse video), not the exited-overlay capsule. SwiftTerm only clears
  selections on streaming output when mouse reporting is ON (`feedPrepare`/`linefeed` guard
  on `allowMouseReporting`). When an agent process exits, its TUI turns mouse reporting OFF,
  so SwiftTerm preserves the selection and it renders over the whole pane. `keyDown`,
  `mouseDown` (single click), `resizeSubviews`, and the ~4s idle-converge refresh each reset
  `selection.active` — which is why it clears when you "work on something else."
- OPEN question that gates the fix: what *creates* the whole-buffer selection at completion.
  Decides between "clear selection on the exited transition" vs. fixing a gesture path.
  Deliberately not fixing on theory (see [[debug-with-live-tests-not-theory]]).
